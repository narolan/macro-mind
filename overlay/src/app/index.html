    How it works

    While a League match is running, MacroMind reads the game's own local data once a second. It never touches the game files and never sends anything over the internet except one download of Riot's public item list.

    Your own stats are exact. The game does not share enemy armor, magic resist or health, so MacroMind estimates them from each champion's base stats, their level and the items they are carrying. Advice that depends on those numbers is marked est.

    The advice comes from plain rules: for example, heavy enemy healing suggests an item that cuts healing. Each suggestion shows its reason. It is a nudge, not an order, and the rules are first guesses.
